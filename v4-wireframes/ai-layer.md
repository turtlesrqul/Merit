# AI Layer

## Principle

AI should search, organize, summarize, and surface relevant proof faster.

AI should not decide who gets hired.

## Approved Uses

AI can help with:

- Extracting skills from projects.
- Summarizing evidence.
- Matching projects to role briefs.
- Finding relevant candidates.
- Explaining why a candidate may be worth reviewing.
- Identifying missing evidence.
- Generating recruiter-readable summaries.
- Helping students improve proof cards.

## Prohibited Uses

AI should not:

- Make hiring decisions.
- Claim someone is objectively better.
- Replace recruiter judgment.
- Create fake scores that look more certain than they are.
- Overpromise fit.
- Use "AI verified" language without a real verification process.
- Hide uncertainty.

## Recommended Wording

| Avoid | Use |
| --- | --- |
| AI Score: 92/100 | Why this candidate may be relevant |
| Best candidate | Strong evidence match |
| AI verified | Evidence found |
| Perfect fit | Relevant proof found |
| Hire recommendation | Review rationale |
| Weak candidate | Missing evidence |

## Recruiter AI Summary

Structure:

1. Why this candidate may be relevant.
2. Strongest matching proof.
3. Skills shown by evidence.
4. Outcome or validation.
5. Missing evidence.
6. Suggested reviewer action.

Example:

> This candidate may be relevant for a frontend intern role because their strongest proof card includes a shipped Next.js dashboard, a live demo, and screenshots showing dashboard states. Evidence supports React, UI implementation, and basic product thinking. Contribution is stated as solo build, but usage metrics are missing.

## Student AI Coach

Student-facing AI should help improve proof:

- "Your contribution is unclear. Add the exact parts you owned."
- "You listed product thinking, but your evidence only shows UI output. Add research notes or decisions."
- "This proof card has a screenshot but no inspectable link. Add a live demo, repo, Figma, or PDF."
- "Your target role is product designer, but the project summary reads like a developer project. Add design process evidence."

## Role Brief Matching

AI should map:

- Role requirements to proof cards.
- Skills to attached evidence.
- Outcomes to recruiter needs.
- Gaps to review caveats.

The output should be a ranked evidence view, not a definitive hiring ranking.

## Trust Rules

- Show source evidence next to AI summary.
- Let recruiters open the proof behind every claim.
- Mark uncertain summaries as "needs review."
- Keep rationale editable or dismissible.
- Never hide the original candidate evidence.

## AI Product Surfaces

### Recruiter Search Result

AI block:

> Why this candidate may be relevant

Contents:

- 2-3 bullets.
- Linked proof cards.
- Evidence level label.
- Missing proof note.

### Candidate Evidence Profile

AI block:

> Evidence summary

Contents:

- Role-specific summary.
- Strongest proof.
- Gaps.
- Suggested interview probe.

### Student Proof Builder

AI block:

> Strengthen this proof

Contents:

- One contribution suggestion.
- One evidence suggestion.
- One outcome suggestion.

## What To Measure

- Recruiter edits or rejects AI summary.
- Recruiter opens cited evidence.
- Recruiter saves/shortlists after summary.
- Student improves proof after AI suggestion.
- Frequency of missing-evidence suggestions.

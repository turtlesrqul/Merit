## Overall Visual System

- Canvas: warm off-white/stone background, mostly flat, no decorative gradients.
- Typography: high-contrast serif display headings with calm sans-serif UI/body text.
- Brand: `Merit` wordmark in italic serif, yellow/gold accent.
- Header: thin top border/line treatment, sparse links, black text, yellow primary CTA.
- Controls: rectangular, hairline black or beige borders, small radius or square edges, yellow primary fill.
- Cards: minimal shadows; use open layouts and hairline divisions rather than heavy SaaS cards.
- Imagery: large, editorial, high-signal project images; project visuals lead the page.
- Grid: generous gutters, three-column desktop project archive, two-column selected works, single-column mobile.
- Motion/decoration: restrained; no orbs, decorative gradients, heavy badges, or dense metadata.
